    Family Narrative

    • Head: Ceily, Cecly, Celia, or Ciley Davis, age 58 in 1900, born Apr 1842 in Georgia, widowed, farmer, and owner of her farm.
    • Maiden-name bridge: The strongest direct identity bridge remains the Brooks County marriage record for Ciley or Celey Lightsey to Richard Davis on 18 Dec 1872.
    • Earlier family household: The 1880 Quitman District household of Richard Davis and Celia Davis with children Warren, Charlotte J., Martha E., and John W. remains the core earlier census anchor.
    • 1900 household structure: Ceily Davis appears in Barney with daughter Jane Leonard and Leonard grandchildren. That is still the best supported reading of the intergenerational household.
    • Later child-forward lead: The 1910 Andrew J. Lennon / Lottie J. Lennon household remains the strongest candidate continuation for daughter Charlotte Jane Davis, but the Leonard/Lennon bridge should still be treated as likely rather than closed.
    • Property significance: Ceily was one of the documented Black property-owning heads in Barney in 1900, an important economic marker in the precinct.
    • Boundary on parentage: The Lightsey surname is directly supported by the marriage record, but claims that Ceily was a daughter of Primus Lightsey and Sylvia Capers-or-Davis remain unproven within this entry.

    Connections to Other Barney Families

    Ceily Davis is tied to the Barney Lightsey network through the 1872 Brooks County marriage record naming her as Ciley or Celey Lightsey when she married Richard Davis. Other Barney households currently discussed as part of the broader Lightsey cluster include William Betts (HH#10), through Ella Betts and Charley Lightsey; Thomas Lightsey (HH#12); Phillip Lightsey (HH#104); Jasper Lightsey (HH#126); and Henry S. Lightsey (HH#165). The connection should remain cautious: Ceily's Lightsey surname is directly supported, but her exact parentage and the precise relationship path to those other Lightsey households have not yet been proved within this entry.
