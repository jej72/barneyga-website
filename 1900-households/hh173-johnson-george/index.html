    12. Sibling Connection to HH#172: The 1860 census confirms George A. Johnson (age 7) in the household of James C. Johnson (age 42) and Sidney L. Mims (age 47), along with James D. Johnson (age 9). Both George and James D. are sons of James C. Johnson, making HH#172 and HH#173 sibling households. Both connect to Rev. Paul Johnson and Mary Polly Hiers (HH#170/HH#144) as grandparents.

    13. Wife's Family: Rhoda Ann Weeks was the daughter of Charles and Rhoda Weeks of Colquitt County. She appears in the 1870 census at age 4 and the 1880 census at age 14 in her father's household. The 1886 marriage in Colquitt County confirms the connection.

    14. County Migration: The family moved from Brooks County (1860-1900) to Colquitt County (1910), a shift of approximately 20 miles. The 1910 census in Robinson, Colquitt County is the last confirmed record.

    15. Name Indexing Variations: The surname appears as "Johnson," "Johhnson" (1880), and "Admson" (1910) across different records — these indexing errors are common for Johnson-family records in this precinct.

    16. Post-1910 Gap: The family has not been found as a complete household in the 1920 or 1930 censuses, nor has a death record been located for George A. Johnson. Rhoda's death record (May 19, 1932, Brooks County GA, Certificate #13278) confirms she survived George, but the timing of his death is unknown. The adult trails for Nancy Johnson Hunt, James Pinkey Johnson, and Daniel S./Shepherd Johnson are now supported by parent-naming death records or death/burial indexes. William, Charley, and George Samuel remain unresolved.

    17. Oregon Record Eliminated: An Oregon death record for "George A Johnson" with spouse "Rhodie E Weeks" and child "George Samuel Johnson" was investigated but eliminated. George would have been ~115 years old if he died in Oregon in 1968, making this a different person with coincidentally similar names.

    Research Status

    Source-researched, not source-exhausted. The George-Rhoda household, marriage, and pre-1910 census trail are supported by primary records. Nancy Johnson Hunt, James Pinkey Johnson, and Daniel S./Shepherd Johnson now have parent-naming adult/death records. June 2026 Ancestry and FamilySearch searches did not locate safe adult trails for William or Charley. George Samuel has a plausible Colquitt-born adult trail, but no parent-naming record was found. The household should not be called source-exhausted until George's death or disappearance, Rhoda's widowhood context, and the remaining adult trails are closed or documented as exhausted.
