    Household Snapshot

    Mitchell Alderman headed a 4-person household in the 1900 Barney census with 1 spouse or marital partner and 2 children.

    Mitchell Alderman's listed occupation was Farmer.

    All listed household members were reported as born in Georgia.

    Connections to Other Barney Families

    • HH#153 Daniel Alderman: The entry currently treats Daniel H. Alderman as Mitchell's half-uncle through John A. Alderman's first marriage to Emily Susannah Norman. This relationship is supported in the current trail mainly by the published Aldermans in America genealogy and should stay marked as secondary until the relevant direct household or probate records are checked.
    • Alderman family cluster: Mitchell belongs in the broader Alderman cluster that includes HH#153, HH#176, HH#178, and HH#180, but the exact kinship paths should be cited from direct records where possible and from Aldermans in America only as secondary support.
    • Crosby-Alderman double marriage: Georgia Crosby's brother Augustus L. Crosby reportedly married Mitchell's sister Martha/Mattie J. Alderman. James did not locate the marriage certificate in this pass, but a Social Security Applications and Claims Index detail page for Mattie J. Alderman names spouse Augustus L. Crosby and child Dennis Wilson Crosby. This supports the couple as a collateral clue, but it is not a marriage record and should not be used to prove the exact marriage date or the full sibling network by itself.

    Research Notes

    • Verify Shelton Madison Alderman and John Franklin Alderman with birth, death, obituary, SS-5, delayed birth, or other records naming parents. The checked 1900 Barney census report and derivative family sources call them sons; direct parent-naming records would strengthen, not reverse, that reading.
    • Verify Georgia Crosby's full sibling list from additional direct census, marriage, probate, or death records rather than relying on Geneanet, FindAGrave family links, or the Social Security Applications and Claims Index collateral clue alone. Her parents, Adam Crosby and Lucy Griffin, are directly supported by her death certificate, and the 1880 census directly places Georgia in Adam and Lucy's household with several likely siblings.
    • Verify Mitchell's siblings, half-siblings, and deeper Alderman lineage from direct census/probate/marriage records. Aldermans in America is useful but remains a secondary genealogy.
    • Cemetery-marker photographs or cemetery register entries would still be useful corroboration, but the original death certificates now directly support Wesley Chapel burial for both Mitchell and Georgia.
    • Corroborate Geraldine Alderman's parentage with a birth, delayed-birth, marriage, death, obituary, or other parent-naming record. The 1930/1940 census chain points to John Franklin Alderman as her father, but a direct parent-naming record would be stronger.
    • Reconcile Mitchell's birth year range: the original death certificate says 20 Nov. 1868, the 1900 census says Nov. 1869, and Geneanet / Aldermans in America give 20 Nov. 1870.

    Open Research Questions

    • Resolve the open items flagged in Research Notes above with direct records before expanding this household narrative.

    Research Status

    • Status: Source-researched, not complete.
    • Strengths: The household has a solid direct-record core: 1900, 1910, 1920, 1930, and Geraldine-linked 1940 census trail; Colquitt County marriage record; and original Georgia death certificates for both Mitchell and Georgia.
    • Remaining cautions: Shelton and John F. are recorded as sons in the checked 1900 Barney census report, so the earlier children-as-stepsons caution was erroneous and should not be preserved. Direct parent-naming records for the two sons would still strengthen the file. Much of the sibling, half-sibling, grandparent, and extended Alderman lineage depends on Aldermans in America, Geneanet, and FindAGrave rather than direct records. Georgia Crosby's parents are now supported by her death certificate and 1880 childhood census household, but her full sibling reconstruction still needs follow-through across later direct records.
