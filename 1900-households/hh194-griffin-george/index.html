    Head of Household

    George Washington Griffin - Born October 7, 1877, Georgia (per WWI draft card; FindAGrave lists February 18, 1877, a discrepancy) - Died December 11, 1961, Colquitt County, Georgia (age 84) - Buried Westview Cemetery, Moultrie, Colquitt County, Georgia (FindAGrave memorial 15964318) - Race: White - Occupation: Saw mill laborer, sawyer, farmer, stock clerk (varied across decades) - Parents: John Griffin (born ~1847, Georgia) and Matilda Griffin (born ~1850, Georgia)

    Parents and Siblings (1880 Census)

    George was one of five children in the John Griffin household at House 162, Quitman, Brooks County: - Samuel Griffin (age 10, born ~1870) - Mary Griffin (age 8, born ~1872) - George Griffin (age 7, born ~1873) - Francis Griffin (age 6, born ~1874) - John Griffin (age 4, born ~1876)

    The 1880 census lists George at age 7 (implying birth ~1873), which conflicts with the October 1877 birth date confirmed by the WWI draft and subsequent censuses. The parents and household location match across sources, so this is likely a census recording error rather than a different individual.

    Marriage

    George married Polly Hewit on October 22, 1899, in Colquitt County, Georgia (Ancestry marriage records, collection 4766, record 290397). The 1900 census lists his wife as "Rollie," which is an indexer misreading of "Pollie" in the original handwriting. Across later records her name appears as Pollie (1910), Kallie (1920, another enumerator variant), and Polly (1930) — all the same person.

    Three Wives

    First Wife: Polly Hewit (married 1899 - died 1932) - Born approximately 1884, Georgia (age 16 in 1900 census) - Married October 22, 1899, Colquitt County, Georgia - Died 1932, Putnam County, Florida (Florida Death Index, collection 7338, record 5105139) - The family lived in Orange Springs, Marion County, Florida in 1930. Putnam County adjoins Marion County to the north, consistent with the family's location.

    Second Wife: Annie B. Manson (married ~1935 - died 1945) - Born approximately 1867 (age 73 in 1940 census) - Died 1945 (per FindAGrave) - George returned to Moultrie, Colquitt County, Georgia by 1935 (per 1940 census residence inference), and appears with Annie as his wife in the 1940 census. - Child with Annie: Myrtle Bell Griffin (1913-1989), who married first Schmidt then Winter. Confirmed by FindAGrave and Social Security Applications and Claims Index.

    Third Wife: Martha Griffin (married ~1945 - survived George) - Born approximately 1880 (age 70 in 1950 census) - Appears as George's wife in the 1950 census in Moultrie, Colquitt County. - George's death in 1961 means Martha likely survived him.

    Children

    With Polly Hewit: - Willie Griffin (born ~1902, Georgia) — appears in 1910 and 1920 censuses - Johnie Griffin (born ~1904, Georgia) — listed as Johmie in 1920; appears in 1910 and 1920 - Earnest Griffin (born ~1906, Georgia) — appears only in 1910 census - Milla Griffin (daughter, born ~1911, Florida) — appears in 1920 census - Mount Jewel Griffin (daughter, born ~1916, Florida) — appears in 1920 census - Mamie Griffin (born ~1916, Florida) — appears in 1930 census

    With Annie B. Manson: - Myrtle Bell Griffin (1913-1989) — married Schmidt, then Winter; per FindAGrave and SS Applications

    Unknown parentage: - Alonzo Griffin (age 39 in 1940 census, born ~1901) — listed as George's son, but not present in any earlier census under that name. May be from a prior marriage, adoption, or a name variation not yet identified.

    Migration Pattern

    George spent his early years in Brooks County, Georgia. The family moved to Madison County, Florida by 1920, then north to Marion County (Orange Springs) by 1930. After Polly's death in 1932, George returned to south Georgia, settling in Moultrie, Colquitt County, where he remained for the rest of his life.

    Notes

    • The 1900 census lists Arthur Chambers (age 22) as a boarder in the household. He does not appear to be related.
    • "Rollie" in 1900 and "Kallie" in 1920 are both indexer misreadings of "Pollie" (Polly Hewit). This has been confirmed by the marriage certificate and the consistent appearance of "Polly" in 1930.
    • The 1880 census age discrepancy (7 vs. the expected 2-3 given an 1877 birth) is notable but the parents and household location match, so this record is included as the same George Griffin.
    • George married three times across a 46-year span. Polly died in Florida; Annie died in Georgia; Martha survived George.
    • George's death at age 84 in Colquitt County closes a life that began in Brooks County, with the marriage to Polly in Colquitt forming a geographic bookend.
