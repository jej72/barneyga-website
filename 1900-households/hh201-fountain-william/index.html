    Notable patterns in this volume

    • Several households connect to established Barney-area family networks, including Alderman, Hiers, Strickland, and related lines.
    • The Black households in this section include farmers, laborers, turpentine workers, sawmill workers, and domestic workers. The census labels are brief, but they still show a varied rural economy.
    • Migration clues remain important. HH#152 is the clearest example in this volume so far, with a likely North Carolina-to-Georgia identification that remains short of final proof.
