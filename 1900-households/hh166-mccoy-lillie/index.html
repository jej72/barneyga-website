    Household #166: McCoy, Lillie

    Back to the 1900 Census & Household Index

    In the 1900 Barney census, Lillie McCoy headed a 2-person household. The table below preserves the exact names, relationships, ages, birthplaces, and occupations recorded for the household.

    Household Snapshot

    Lillie McCoy headed a 2-person household in the 1900 Barney census with 1 boarder, employee, servant, or other non-kin resident.

    Lillie McCoy's listed occupation was Dressmaker.

    All listed household members were reported as born in Georgia.

    Children and Later Trails

    No children are listed in this household in the 1900 census table.

    The 1900 household is unusual because Lillie was recorded as a married sixteen-year-old head of household with no husband present. The surviving local source extracts now support the older identification of this woman as Lollie/Lottie Chambers McCoy: FamilySearch marriage-index extracts for Joseph McCoy and Lollie/Lottie Chambers give a Brooks County marriage on 11 March 1900, and the 1910 census places Joe W. McCoy and Lollie McCoy together in Barney with children Georgie, Oma L., Clara B., Gordon, Talmage, and Howard. The marriage date and the 1910 census marriage-duration statement should still be handled cautiously, because the 1910 index says the couple had been married eleven years, while the indexed marriage date is March 1900.

    Lillie/Lollie’s parentage is supported by the FamilySearch death-index extract for Mrs. J. W. McCoy, wife of Joseph William McCoy. It records her death on 17 December 1928 in District 637, Thomas County, Georgia, age forty-four, born Georgia, certificate #34540, and lists James Chambers and Nettie Webb as other people on the record. That supports the connection to James G. Chambers of HH#198. A saved FamilySearch image-page screenshot exists for the death certificate, but the OCR is imperfect; the indexed extract is currently the clearer local source.

    Joseph William McCoy’s identity is now stronger than in the older entry. FamilySearch and Ancestry extracts give a 1 February 1883 birth date, with the WWI draft registration placing the birth in Brooks County, Georgia and naming Lollie McCoy as spouse in the Ancestry extract. The 1900 Ancestry census extract places Joe McCoy, born February 1883, as a son in John and Hester McCoy’s household, and the FamilySearch NUMIDENT extract names Joseph William McCoy’s parents as John B. McCoy and Hester A. Strickland. This gives direct support for the HH#117 John Buck McCoy and HH#32 William M. C. McCoy cross-volume line, though the HH#117/HH#32 entries should remain the main place for the full parentage chain.

    There were no children in Lillie’s 1900 household. The child trails belong to the continuing Joe and Lollie/Katie McCoy family documented in later records. The 1910 census records Joe and Lollie with six children. The 1920 census records Joe W. and wife Katie, likely the same woman under a variant or indexing problem, with Belle, Gordon, Talmage, Howard, Rosie, Agnis, and Herman. By 1930, after Lollie’s 1928 death, Joe appears with wife Mary, son Herman, daughter Rosa M., son Carden/Gordon, and Manning stepchildren or household children. In 1940, Joe W. McCoy is in George B. and Clara Hunt’s Brooks County household as father-in-law, with Stella McCoy also present. In 1950, widowed Joseph W. McCoy is in St. Petersburg, Pinellas County, Florida, as father of Arthur H. McCoy. A Florida death-index extract records Joseph W. McCoy’s death in Pinellas County in February 1965, certificate #10042.

A separate 1916 Georgie Mae McCoy/Watt Jenkins marriage and 1920/1930/1940 Georgia Jenkins census chain in Colquitt County is a strong candidate trail, but the visible extracts reviewed so far do not name her parents. The 1919 muster-roll lead is suspicious because HH#166 Talmage was only about 10–12 then. Open; adult identity not currently closeable.
    Howard / William Howard McCoy Howard appears in 1910 and 1920. A FamilySearch marriage extract records Wm Howard Mc Coy, age 25, son of Lollie McCoy and Joe W.     Rosa / Rosie M. McCoy Glow Rosie appears in 1920 and Rosa M. appears in Joe’s 1930 household. A FamilySearch marriage extract records Rosa Mae McCoy, age 24, daughter of Lollie McCoy and J. W. Strengthened adult trail; later census/death still open.
    Agnes / Agnis McCoy Jenkins Agnis appears as a daughter in 1920, age 6. A FamilySearch marriage extract records Agnes McCoy, age 18, daughter of Lollie McCoy and Joe McCoy, marrying Patt Jenkins in District 1151, Colquitt County, on 2 September 1928. A focused 2026-06-09 public/newspaper search found no proof-level later census/death/obit/burial hit. Strengthened; later census/death still open; the older A. H. McCoy/Fay Lewis groom lead is not used for this daughter and should be treated cautiously.
    Arthur Herman McCoy Herman appears as a young son in 1920 and 1930. A FamilySearch WWII draft-registration extract identifies Arthur Herman McCoy, born 26 February 1914 in Brooks County, and the 1950 census places Joseph W. McCoy in Arthur H. McCoy’s St. Petersburg household as father. Browser-backed FamilySearch follow-up extracted NUMIDENT 6KSB-Q311VV73-2DH for Arthur Herman Mccoy, born 06 Feb 1914, died 9 Nov 1972 in Pinellas County, Florida, certificate #76029. Strong adult trail with index-level death evidence; parent/spouse-naming death certificate or obituary still needed.

    Mary Studstill remains unresolved. The 1900 boarder was about 24 and born in Georgia. Candidate review rejects Mrs. Mary S. Studstill, born about 1845, as too old; Mary E. Studstill in Wilcox County and Mollie Studstill in Berrien County are age-compatible but not tied directly to Brooks/Barney or to McCoy/Chambers records. Lollie Chambers McCoy's sister Vesta Chambers later married/lived with Hugh Studstill in Valdosta, but that is contextual only and too late to identify the 1900 boarder. Mary should be treated as an associated boarder, not as a proven relative of Lillie or Joseph.
