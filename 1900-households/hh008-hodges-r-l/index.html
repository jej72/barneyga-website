    Household #8: Hodges, R L

    Back to the 1900 Census & Household Index

    In the 1900 Barney census, R. L. Hodges headed a 10-person household. The table below preserves the exact names, relationships, ages, birthplaces, and occupations recorded for the household.

    Household Snapshot

    R. L. Hodges was listed in 1900 as a 64-year-old Georgia-born farmer. His household belongs to the farm economy around Barney, where land, crops, livestock, kin labor, and seasonal work still shaped daily life alongside the newer railroad town.

    The 1900 household combined the older Hodges couple, three unmarried adult Hodges children, and the Culbreth son-in-law, daughter, and grandchildren. The page is a two-generation household record, with the Culbreth family already tied into the Hodges line through Effie V. Hodges Culbreth.

    Every listed person in the household was reported as born in Georgia. Later records are still needed before claiming movement into or out of Barney for any individual member.

    Newspaper and Public-Record Trail

    • The Valdosta Times reported on 1 December 1900 that R. L. Hodges died suddenly during cane-stripping work on Tuesday, 27 November 1900. The item says he complained of being tired, rested on a fence, fell a few minutes later, and was found dead. That article supplies the contemporary newspaper context for the death date preserved in the family narrative below.
    • A 2 January 1909 Valdosta Daily Times social note says Miss Hattie Hodges had returned to her home at Barney after visiting Miss Vesta Chambers in Valdosta. This is probably the Harriett Hodges listed in this 1900 household, but the notice itself gives only the shortened name and Barney residence, so it should remain a likely identification rather than a closed proof.

    Verified Identity: Robinson L. (Robert) Hodges

    Full genealogy confirmed via Huxford's History of Brooks County (pp. 484–485) and Find A Grave (Memorial 111611356):

    • Father: James Carr Hodges (b. Mar 18, 1800, Bulloch Co., GA — d. Jan 17, 1865, Brooks Co., GA); buried Hodges Cemetery, Brooks Co. (Find A Grave 106716862); Indian War veteran (Capt. Levi J. Knight's independent company, 1838); Captain of Militia, 659th district, commissioned Apr 22, 1844; lived on lot 552, 13th district, Brooks Co.
    • Mother: Sarah Ann Newton (b. Jun 3, 1802 — d. Sep 18, 1893); daughter of George Newton; buried Hodges Cemetery (Find A Grave 106716833)
    • Wife: Mary J. Stephens (m. Jan 22, 1861)

    Parents' Birthplace: 1880 census records both father's and mother's birthplace as North Carolina.

    Siblings: 1. James Lafayette "Fate" Hodges (b. 1829 — d. 1890) — moved to Florida 2. Sarah Adeline Hodges (b. 1832) — m. William Alfred Robeson; d. 1883 3. Joseph Jackson Hodges (b. Jan 15, 1836 — d. Jul 26, 1910) — prominent farmer, 3,000 acres, owned store/sawmill/gristmill/cotton gins; buried Hodges Cemetery (Find A Grave 106717207); m. Rachel Caroline Delk (Nov 11, 1866); 11 children 4. Mary C. Hodges (b. 1838 — d. 1926) — m. Tobias G. Robinson, May 28, 1857; buried Hodges Cemetery 5. Margaret Hodges (b. 1840) — m. J. Cobb 6. Martha A. "Mattie" Hodges (b. 1842 — d. 1924) — m. Berrien Beaty; buried Hodges Cemetery

    Grandfather: Joseph Hodges (b. 1758) — married (1) Elenor Denmark; (2) Sarah Carr

    Great-grandfather: Joshua Hodges (b. Oct 13, 1736, NC — d. Mar 13, 1809, Bulloch Co., GA) — Revolutionary War soldier; enlisted from Martin Co., NC under Capt. Kenneth McKenzie, 1780; granted land in Burke Co., GA for service; married Ann Raiford, 1756

    Death: November 27, 1900, Brooks County, Georgia. A 1 December 1900 Valdosta Times item reports that he died suddenly during cane-stripping work after the 1900 census enumeration.

    Burial: Camp Ground Cemetery, Mount Zion Church, Morven, Brooks County, Georgia (Find A Grave 111611356). Note: Huxford references Hodges Cemetery, but FindAGrave confirms burial at Camp Ground.

    Civic Roles: - Founding trustee, Barney Methodist Church (1899) — listed among early Barney settler families in Huxford

    Notable Relatives: Brother Joseph J. Hodges was among the wealthiest farmers in northern Brooks County (Okapilco–Tallokas section); started with $300 capital, accumulated 3,000 acres plus store, sawmill, gristmill, and cotton gins; stockholder in banks at Pavo and Quitman.

    Culbreth Family: J.B. Culbreth (Joseph B. Culbreth, b. ~1867, Savannah, Chatham Co., GA) married Effie V. Hodges, daughter of R.L. and Mary J. Hodges. J.B. worked as a saw mill laborer in Barney. The Culbreth family was not established in Brooks County — no references in Huxford; left Barney District by 1910 (no Culbreth surname in 1910 census for Barney). Joseph B. Culbreth's parents are unknown (Ancestry tree says "Unknown"). The family likely came to Barney for saw mill work.

    Effie V Hodges note: An unsourced Ancestry tree (Art Stewart's Ancestors 2025) links Effie to "Redding Othaniel Augustus Hodges Jr." (1837-1904, Sandersville, Washington Co., GA). This is incorrect — Redding Jr.'s family is entirely Washington County and unrelated to Brooks County. R.L. Hodges is Effie's actual father per the 1900 census (which lists her as "Daughter" in his household). The Ancestry tree likely conflated two different Effie Hodges from different branches.

    1850 Census (Lowndes County, GA): Robertson Hodges, age 14, in household of James C Hodges (50) and Sarah A Hodges (47). Siblings: Lafayette (21), Adeline (18), Joseph J (15), Mary (12), Margaret (10), Martha (7). Robertson was attending school.

    1860 Census (Brooks County, GA): Robinson L Hodges, age 24, farmer, still in father J.C. Hodges' household at Tallokas PO. Not yet married.

    1860 Census — Wife's Family (Brooks County, GA): Mary J Stevens, age 18, in household of Cadar Stevens (53) and Levina Stevens (46). Siblings: Florida G, Richard C (16), Harriet A (14), Francis (6), Andrew (4), George F.

    Marriage Certificate: Robinson Hodges married Mary J. Stephens on 22 January 1861 in Brooks County, Georgia. Confirmed by two independent sources (Ancestry 7839/92013 and Ancestry 4766/182273). Note: some sources incorrectly state 1864.

    1870 Census (Brooks County, GA): Robinson Hodges, age 34, farm laborer, Quitman PO. Household: Mary (30, wife), Thomas (8, son), Sarah (4, daughter), Mary (3, daughter).

    1880 Census (Morven, Brooks County, GA): R.L. Hodges, age 44, farmer. Mary J. Hodges (41, wife). Children in household: Thomas J. (16), Sarah L. (14), Mary A. (12), James H. (10), Margarette E. (8), Harriet A. (6), Emma F. (4), Joseph E. (2), plus unnamed infant (~1880). Father's birthplace: North Carolina. Mother's birthplace: North Carolina.

    1900 Census: R.L. Hodges (64, head, b. Jan 1836 GA), Mary J. (62, wife), John H (29, son), Harriett (26, daughter), Joseph E (22, son). R.L. was alive at enumeration (June 1, 1900) and died five months later.

    1910 Census (LaGrange, Troup County, GA): Mary J. Hodges, age 71, widowed, living alone on Ware Street. Father's birthplace: North Carolina. Mother's birthplace: Georgia. After R.L.'s death in 1900, Mary moved from Brooks County to LaGrange.

    Connections to Other Barney Families

    • The page documents a direct Hodges-Culbreth connection inside HH#8: Effie V. Hodges, daughter of R. L. and Mary J. Hodges, was married to J. B. Culbreth, and their children James, Carrodine, and Bessie Culbreth were listed as Hodges grandchildren in the same household.
    • No separate Culbreth household has been identified elsewhere in the 1900 Barney census list on the current page evidence.
    • J. B. Culbreth may be related to Prof. B. H. Culbreth, the Barney school figure identified in 1901-1902 newspaper items, but proof of that relationship has not been found at this time.
