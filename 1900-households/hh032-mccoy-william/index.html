    Family Narrative

    • Head: William M. C. McCoy, age 69 in 1900, born Sep 1830 in Georgia, farmer.
    • Wife: Elizabeth A. Seay McCoy, born 25 Dec 1834 in South Carolina, later dying 6 Nov 1921 in Colquitt County, Georgia.
    • 1900 household facts: Married about 40 years, living in Barney, William reported both parents born in Georgia, and the farm was owned free of mortgage.
    • Census chain: The preserved record trail supports William across 1850, 1860, 1870, 1880, 1900, 1910, and 1920, creating a strong long-run identity chain in south Georgia.
    • Marriage: User-supplied marriage-record images support the Brooks County marriage of William McCoy and Elizabeth A. See or Seay on 26 Jul 1860.
    • Military trail: User-supplied Confederate service images and pension-index material support service in Company H, 9th Georgia Infantry and later Colquitt County pension activity.
    • Burial and death-side support: Elizabeth's Georgia death certificate and the two Pleasant Hill Cemetery memorials strongly support the later Colquitt County burial trail.
    • Name-form caution: The military or burial trail sometimes uses William N. McCoy rather than William M. C. McCoy. The spouse, county, regiment, and burial context suggest the same man, but the name-form conflict should stay visible.
