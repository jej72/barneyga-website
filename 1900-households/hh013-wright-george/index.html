    Family Narrative

    • Head: George Wright, age 24, born about May 1876 in Georgia. In 1900 he was a day laborer at a sawmill in Barney, Brooks County, Georgia.

    Wife: Lucy Wright, age 20, born January 1880 in Georgia. Lucy was Lucy Lightsey before marriage. She is securely identified as the daughter of Thomas Lightsey and Elizabeth Lightsey of HH#12.

    Children identified from verified household records: - Allice Wright, daughter, age 3 in 1900, born about 1897 in Georgia. - Thomas Wright, son, age 1 in 1900, born about 1899 in Georgia. - Alice B. Wright, daughter, age 14 in 1910, born about 1896 in Georgia. - Tommie Wright, son, age 12 in 1910, born about 1898 in Georgia. - Eva May Wright, daughter, age 4 in 1910 and Eva Wright, daughter, age 14 in 1920, born about 1905-1906. - Jessie or Jesse J. Wright, son, age 10 in 1920 and age 20 in 1930, born about 1909-1910 in Florida.

    Verified household path: - 1880: Lucy Lightsey appears in Quitman, Brooks County, Georgia, in the household of Thomas and Elizabeth Lightsey as their infant daughter. - 30 Sep 1896: George Wright married Lucy Lightsey in Brooks County, Georgia. - 1900: George and Lucy Wright appear in Barney, Brooks County, next to the Lightsey household, with children Allice and Thomas. - 1910: George and Lucy appear in Nixon, Washington County, Florida, with Alice B., Tommie, and Eva May. - 1920: George and Lucy appear in Newark, Essex County, New Jersey, at 21 Alling Street, with Eva and Jessie. - 1930: Lucy Wright appears in Newark as a widowed head of household with son Jesse J. Wright. - March 1937: Lucy Wright filed a Social Security application under the alias Lucy Lightsey, giving her birth as 19 Jan 1880 in Quitman, Georgia, and naming her father as Thomas Lightsey and her mother as Elizabeth Hurnur as indexed.

    Assessment:

    The link between HH#12 and HH#13 is proven. Lucy Lightsey of the Thomas Lightsey household is the same Lucy Wright who appears in 1900 Barney, 1910 Washington County, 1920 Newark, 1930 Newark, and the 1937 NUMIDENT record. The family moved from Brooks County into the Florida turpentine belt and then to Newark during the early twentieth-century labor migration.

    George Wright is securely identified as Lucy's husband through the 1920 census. After that point, the record becomes less certain. The strongest current reading is that George either died or was otherwise absent before the 1930 census, because Lucy appears in Newark as a widow. A 1930 George Wright in Wilkes County, Georgia, and later 1940 and 1950 Georgia candidates remain unproven and should not be folded into this household without a direct bridge.

    Connections to treat cautiously:

    Lucy is tied to HH#12 through primary records. Wider claims linking this household to the Betts family or to a specific Flournoy sibling group remain unresolved and should not be stated as proven. George's parentage also remains unsettled. A FamilySearch tree and a plausible 1880 Brooks County household suggest Berry Wright and Catharine Mahaila Lampkins as candidate parents, but a World War I draft card from Newark names George's nearest relative as his mother, Mrs. Sallie Gibson, in Georgia. No direct record yet resolves that conflict.

    Connections to Other Barney Families
