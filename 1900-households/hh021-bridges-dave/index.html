    Family Narrative

    • Census Page: 3 | Lines: 1-3
    • Head: Dave Bridges (age 20, Georgia, laborer at saw mill) — born October 1879, Georgia
    • Spouse: Cassie Bridges (wife, age 23, Georgia) — born about 1877
    • Marriage: 22 May 1898, Berrien County, Georgia, to Cassie/Cossie Wilker or Wilkes
    • Children:
    • John D. Bridges (son, age 1) — born about 1899

    Identity and family reconstruction - The 1900 census records this household under the name Dave Bridges and ties the entry to index variants that include Downie Bridges. - A Berrien County marriage record dated 22 May 1898 names Downie Bridges as the groom and Cassie/Cossie Wilker as the bride. - A World War I draft registration card for Donnie Bridges, signed Downie Bridges, gives residence at Berlin, Georgia, birth date 23 October 1879, and nearest relative Cassie Bridges. - Later family records connect Willard D. or Willard Downey Bridges to wife Nancy Wilkes/Nancy C. Bridges and son John Durell Bridges. Those records include a FindAGrave memorial for Nancy C. Bridges and the death certificate of son Bruce Burton Bridges, which names his parents as Willard D. Bridges and Nancy Wilkes. - Taken together, these records support identifying the 1900 household head Dave Bridges as the same man later recorded as Downie/Donnie Bridges and Willard D./Willard Downey Bridges.

    Parentage and sibling context - An 1880 census household in Powder Springs, Cobb County, Georgia, lists T. M. Bridges and S. L. Bridges with children including J. M. Bridges, age 3, and D. Bridges, age 7/12. - This household fits the later evidence well and strongly supports identifying Dave/Downie/Willard as a son of Thomas M. Bridges and Sarah L. Bridges, later expanded in other records as Thomas Marion Bridges and Sarah Louisa Dean Bridges. - The same 1880 household also provides the strongest preserved support that HH#21 Dave Bridges and HH#27 James Marvin Bridges were brothers.

    Later residence and death - Later records place the family in south Georgia and eventually in Chatham County. - A Chatham County death index entry records Willard D. Bridges, age 70, dying on 30 March 1949 under certificate 4514.

    Connections to Other Barney Families

    • HH#21 to HH#27 James Marvin Bridges: The preserved 1880 Cobb County household and later records strongly support the conclusion that Dave Bridges of HH#21 and James Marvin Bridges of HH#27 were brothers.
